Tree Small 02 is distributed as two reduced, derived geometry-only GLBs: near 79,263 triangles / 3,877,092 bytes and far 14,503 triangles / 809,676 bytes. Geometry plus the seven unchanged shared maps totals 8,438,382 bytes, excluding small manifests and licenses. The original 95,102,324-byte tree_small_02.bin is not distributed; its SHA256 is 8da6c3c389ad8748286d1b7488cd827f75ebdad9d8f59ef7dfb0916df5edc634.
Offline adaptation uses glTF-Transform 4.5.1 vertex welding and meshoptimizer 1.3.0 attribute-aware simplification. Reduction is lossy and can change components, detail and silhouette. Used source UV transforms are baked into the exported UVs. No runtime compression codec is required.
The original leaf atlas and separate PNG alpha mask are unchanged. Generic seamless Bark Brown 01 replaces the source branch/trunk bark artistically. The model is scaled to 25 selected real OSM tree positions; its shape and species are not a survey of Naples vegetation. Provenance and adaptation details: foliage/tree_small_02/tree-lod-manifest.json and TREE-MODEL-LICENSE.txt.

Three selected regions of the same daylight photograph are projected on the confirmed Royal Continental, Grand Hotel Vesuvio and Grand Hotel Santa Lucia OSM fronts (relation/4779351:1, way/337012555:0, relation/1191265:0). Three-dimensional recesses, frames and balconies are inferred artwork, not photogrammetry. Other building fronts use generic materials.
The Santa Lucia four-arch portico is original inferred geometry, replacing only the rendering of the exact untagged 10 m proxy way/338157976:0. Inferred slab top 3.84 m and balustrade cap 4.416 m are relative to the building base, not measured architectural heights. Source footprint, source height and collision geometry are unchanged.
