Tree Small 02 is distributed as two reduced, derived geometry-only GLBs: near 299,060 triangles / 14,695,012 bytes and far 119,846 triangles / 7,147,048 bytes. Geometry plus the seven unchanged shared maps totals 25,593,674 bytes, excluding small manifests and licenses. The original 95,102,324-byte tree_small_02.bin is not distributed; its SHA256 is 8da6c3c389ad8748286d1b7488cd827f75ebdad9d8f59ef7dfb0916df5edc634.
Offline adaptation uses glTF-Transform 4.5.1 vertex welding and meshoptimizer 1.3.0 attribute-aware simplification. All 30,250 connected leaf components remain in each LOD; these are not necessarily individual botanical leaves. Offline measured source alpha-covered surface area at the runtime cutoff is 71.24% near and 44.38% far; diagnostic targets 90%/70% are not met. Reduction is lossy and changes area, detail and silhouette. Used source UV transforms are baked into the exported UVs. No runtime compression codec is required.
The original leaf atlas and separate PNG alpha mask are unchanged. Generic seamless Bark Brown 01 replaces the source branch/trunk bark artistically. The model is scaled to 25 selected real OSM tree positions; its shape and species are not a survey of Naples vegetation. Provenance and adaptation details: foliage/tree_small_02/tree-lod-manifest.json and TREE-MODEL-LICENSE.txt.

The Santa Lucia four-opening portico is inferred geometry with a flat canopy and coping, without an invented roof balustrade. It replaces only the rendering of the exact untagged 10 m proxy way/338157976:0. Source footprint, source height and collision geometry are unchanged. Five upper photographic window registers, including the attic, are distinct from the OSM six-level tag and unchanged 19.2 m height. Higher rails are dark iron; the lowest upper terrace uses continuous stone balustrade. Dimensions and depths are inferred, not measured architectural heights.
