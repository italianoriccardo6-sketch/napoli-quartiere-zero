This is a derived database. Source geometry, attribution, checksums, tile lineage,
the public source download, and extraction steps are recorded in source.json.
The source archive is publicly available at the pinned URL in source.json;
scripts/prepare-photo-world.mjs rebuilds this derivative. The geometry's ODbL
terms remain applicable regardless of the source project's software license.

Source package: Napoli / NAP, Maximilian284/subwaybuilder-it-maps, release 1.4.0,
city version 1.1.0, dated 2026-07-18, packaged by Massimiliano Prandi.
The source project's MIT license is reproduced in source-project-MIT.txt.

The source basemap identifies OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth. Natural Earth is
public domain; this derivative reads zoom-15 layers, whose prepared coast/earth
data is based on OpenStreetMap. Photography and Google meshes are not extracted,
copied, analyzed, or included in this database.

This is cartographic geometry, not surveyed terrain or an assurance of complete
coverage, road clearance, present-day accessibility, or verified building height.

The coverage clip uses the Napoli municipal statistical boundary under CC-BY-4.0:
Fonte: Istat, confini amministrativi non generalizzati al 1 gennaio 2026;
conversione CRS e distribuzione l1n00/confini-comuni-istat; ritaglio cartografico al

sea classification. See public/data/napoli-boundary-source.json for its lineage.
